# Walmart-Sales-ML
# About the Project
This project analyzes the Walmart Weekly Sales dataset to support store-level operational planning. Our goal is to understand how weekly sales change during holiday weeks and identify which factors most strongly influence weekly sales across stores.

We first conduct exploratory data analysis (EDA) to compare sales in holiday vs. non-holiday weeks and rank stores by their holiday sales uplift (i.e., how much sales increase during holiday periods). Next, we apply machine learning (Random Forest) to model weekly sales and extract feature importance to explain the main drivers of sales, including Holiday_Flag, Temperature, Fuel_Price, CPI, and Unemployment.

The project outputs actionable insights such as:
- the most holiday-sensitive stores (highest uplift),
- the top drivers of weekly sales overall and for selected high-sales stores,
and visualizations that help translate results into planning decisions (e.g., when to prepare more replenishment/capacity/staffing).
